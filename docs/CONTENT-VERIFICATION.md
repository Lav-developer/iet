# Content verification and institutional handover

## Primary source

`IET26072026.pdf` supplied for this implementation, 20 pages. It is the primary source for IET-specific seed content.

## Secondary source

The public DSMNRU website was used to identify university-owned routes and the university contact for the Faculty of Engineering & Technology. It is not used to invent IET facts.

## Seeded as published

- IET established in 2016.
- The transition described from IET to a broader Faculty of Engineering & Technology.
- Vision and mission statements from pages 1–2.
- Six department names and department profiles from pages 3 and 7–20.
- Programme names, levels, durations and approved seats from Table 1, page 2.
- Dean profile and research areas from page 6.
- Faculty and laboratory/non-teaching staff records explicitly listed in pages 7–20.
- Laboratory/facility headings explicitly named in the department sections.
- Research-area records derived from the Dean and ECE profile language.
- Official DSMNRU links for the university site, Samarth, notices, results, admissions and the source IET PDF.

## Intentionally not asserted

- Current eligibility rules and fee amounts beyond the source seat matrix.
- Placement percentages, rankings, accreditations, collaborations or research metrics.
- A complete faculty count where the PDF gives a summary count but does not provide all detailed profiles.
- Equipment, laboratory capacity, room locations or safety instructions.
- Current events, achievements, publications, projects, student organisations or alumni activities.
- Postal address details not confirmed as IET-specific.
- Final privacy, grievance, accessibility or analytics policy language.
